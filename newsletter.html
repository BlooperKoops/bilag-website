About the Newsletter

The BILAG newsletter shares updates on lupus research activity, trial opportunities, registry and biologics work, education/events, digital tool developments, and key group announcements.

Subscribers receive periodic summary emails highlighting new resources and upcoming opportunities relevant to clinicians, researchers, and collaborators.
