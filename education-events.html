BILAG Advanced Course in SLE (2026)

The inaugural BILAG Advanced Course on Systemic Lupus Erythematosus took place in York on 30-31 January 2026, bringing together expert-led lectures and interactive workshops for UK clinicians and multidisciplinary teams.

The programme was designed to strengthen clinical decision-making and implementation of contemporary SLE care, with the updated 2026 BSR whole life-course guideline as a core framework.

Sessions highlighted practical disease assessment, patient perspective contributions, advances in lupus nephritis and biologic strategy, and collaborative quality improvement in UK services.

This course reflects BILAG's ongoing commitment to translating evidence into better outcomes and equitable care for people living with lupus.
