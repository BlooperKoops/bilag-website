Group Photo Placeholder

BILAG exists to advance the accurate, consistent, and meaningful assessment of lupus disease activity. The group develops and refines validated measurement tools, promotes education and training, and supports high-quality research to improve clinical care and trial outcomes in systemic lupus erythematosus.
