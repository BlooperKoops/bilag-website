BILAG Membership

Who we are, and how to apply.

BILAG membership is selective and intended for clinicians and researchers with a demonstrable, sustained, and high-impact record in lupus research. Applications are considered only where there is clear evidence of ongoing leadership in lupus assessment, clinical investigation, or related scholarship.

Applications, comprising an academic CV and a brief letter of intent (or equivalent supporting statement) outlining your contributions and reasons for applying, should be submitted through an existing BILAG member or the BILAG committee.
